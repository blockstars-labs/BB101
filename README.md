## In English

**BB101** is our build log for **Blockchain Builders 101**, the Stellar bootcamp run by the Blockchain Acceleration Foundation (BAF). Over five weeks we go from a real problem to a working prototype on Stellar testnet: identify, design, build, integrate and demo.

We start where trust breaks down in Latin America. Our first week maps four problems from very different worlds:

- **Capital markets:** funds that cannot settle trades without exposing their price, size and counterparty.
- **Construction:** families and contractors who risk upfront payments with no guarantee on either side.
- **Nonprofits:** field teams paying in cash to vendors who issue no invoice, and donors who cannot verify the spend.
- **Agriculture:** lenders and buyers who cannot verify, from afar, that a physical asset like cattle exists and is in the state reported.

We are now narrowing them down to one. Our individual write-ups (in Spanish, as the bootcamp requires) live in [`docs/semana1`](docs/semana1).

**Team:** [Emanuel Benavides León](https://github.com/EmanuXBe) and [Jairo Enrique Amaya Celis](https://github.com/jairoamayac), builders from Colombia 🇨🇴.
